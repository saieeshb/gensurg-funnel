== 17. HOW MUCH OF REALITY DOES THIS SHEET SEE?  (coverage & bias)
============================================================================================
Reported invites: 1154 | distinct programs appearing: 315
Sheet's own count of programs that released invites: 498

Order-of-magnitude coverage (external anchors, not from the sheet):
  General Surgery categorical PGY-1 positions per year ~1,700-1,800 across ~350 programs.
  At a typical ~10-12 interviews offered per position, national invite volume is ~18,000-22,000.
  => this sheet captures roughly 6% of categorical invites (order 5-6%).

Direction of the biases this creates:
  1. VOLUNTEER bias — people post when something happens. Non-events (no invite) are invisible.
     The sheet therefore cannot estimate any absolute probability, only compositions.
  2. SUCCESS bias — an applicant with 15 invites can post 15 times; one with 0 posts never.
     Records are weighted toward successful applicants, inflating apparent score/credential levels.
  3. SURVIVOR bias in later waves — those still posting in December are those still searching.
  4. AUDIENCE bias — the sheet skews US-MD and US-based; Non-US IMG behaviour is under-sampled
     relative to their true applicant share (13% of records here).
  5. RECALL/format drift — free text; ~2% of fields unparseable, and "no signal" may be
     under-reported by people who only bother to note a signal when they have one.

What this means: read every number here as "among invites that were reported", and lean on
the tier-matched and within-sheet contrasts (which share the bias on both sides) rather than levels.
